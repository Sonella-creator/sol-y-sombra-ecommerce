Born under a serious sun

Sol & Sombra started as a beach stall in Onda, Castellón — a folding table, a mirror, and thirty pairs of sunglasses that sold out before lunch. The Mediterranean gets over 300 days of sunshine a year, so we learned quickly: shade is not a luxury, it's equipment.

Today we curate frames the same way we did on that first morning. Each style has to earn its place three ways:

  • Protection first. Every lens is UV400 rated, no exceptions.
  • Character second. If a frame doesn't have a personality, it doesn't get a name.
  • Fair prices always. From €19 fiesta shades to €120 shields — nothing gathers dust.

Frequently asked

Are the lenses really UV400?
Yes — every pair, from the cheapest to the most expensive, blocks 99–100% of UVA and UVB light.
Do you ship outside Spain?
We ship across the UK and EU. Orders over €60 ship free.
What if the frame doesn't suit me?
Free returns within 30 days, no questions, no sighing.
Can I try before I buy?
Our stall still opens every summer weekend on the Castellón coast — come and say hola.
